Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fractional CTO?

A senior technology executive who works with you part-time and carries full CTO responsibility. Also called a part-time CTO or CTO as a service.

When should a Singapore company hire a fractional CTO?

After a vendor project underperforms, before a fundraise, or while searching for a permanent CTO.

What does technical due diligence cover?

Code, architecture, infrastructure, security, data handling, team and remediation cost.

Do you work with companies outside Singapore?

Yes. We are based in Singapore and work across Southeast Asia.
